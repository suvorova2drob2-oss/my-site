Connected speech: intrusive sounds

Tracks 5.3 and 5.4 — /w/, /j/ and /r/ links between vowel sounds.

← Unit 5

1 Track 5.3 — examples

In connected speech, an extra, ‘intrusive’ sound is sometimes added to link vowel sounds in two different words. Listen and repeat.

1 Trying to ensure our teenage children do their fair share is no easy task. /w/

2 But it’s best to try and avoid them before they actually happen. /j/

3 … the chore itself is not negotiable … the job gets done sooner or later. /r/

2 SPEAK

Work in pairs. Look again at the examples in Exercise 1 and discuss when /w/, /j/ and /r/ are added in connected speech.
