Ex. 5 — Meaning pairs

Explain the difference in meaning. Tap a card to reveal the Teacher’s Book notes.

1a to work part time  /  1b to work full time
Part time: contracted for fewer hours than full time (e.g. 21 hours/week). Full time: contracted for the entire time appropriate to that job (e.g. 35 hours/week).
2a to work overtime  /  2b to work long hours
Overtime: work supplementary hours for which you are paid extra. Long hours: work for many hours each day (not necessarily paid as overtime).
3a to work flexitime  /  3b to work shifts
Flexitime: flexible timetable — within limits you decide when you start and finish, as long as you work the required total. Shifts: work a set period (e.g. 12 am–8 am) before other workers replace you for the next period.
