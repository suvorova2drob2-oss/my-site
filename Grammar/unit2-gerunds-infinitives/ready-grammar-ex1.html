1 Complete the sentences

Some verbs can be followed by a gerund and some others can be followed by an infinitive with to. Complete the sentences with the correct form of the verbs in brackets. One verb in each sentence will be a gerund, the other an infinitive with to.

Patterns

Gerund verb + -ing — e.g. going, smiling, buying

Infinitive with to to + verb — e.g. to meet, to hit, to enjoy

In each sentence above, one gap needs -ing, the other needs to + verb.
