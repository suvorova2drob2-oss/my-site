1 SPEAK Why are so and such used in the following sentences? What types of words follow so and such?
I was so impressed with the soundtrack of the film that I downloaded it as soon as I got home.
She has such a wonderful voice that it seems a shame to dub her films into English.
After you discuss — grammar note

Both words intensify the adjective or adjective + noun that follow. so + adjective (or adverb); such + (indefinite article +) adjective + noun.
