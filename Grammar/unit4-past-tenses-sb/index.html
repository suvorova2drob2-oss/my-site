4 Name the tenses and explain the difference

1a When he was having breakfast, he read the newspaper.
1b When he’d had breakfast, he read the newspaper.

2a I heard about it when I was listening to the news on the radio.
2b I listened to the news on the radio when I heard about it.

3a I lived in Oxford for six years.
3b I had been living in Oxford for six years.

1a past continuous + past simple (he read during breakfast). 1b past perfect + past simple (he read after breakfast). 2a past simple + past continuous (heard it while listening). 2b past simple + past simple (heard it, and as a result listened). 3a past simple (the speaker no longer lives in Oxford). 3b past perfect continuous (situation before another past event; we do not know if the speaker still lives there).
