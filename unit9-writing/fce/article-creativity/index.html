6 Write your article (140–190 words)

Why is it important for teachers to inspire their students?

Is there more to teaching than knowing your subject? How can teachers encourage student interest in their lessons? Why is this important for students?

The best articles will be posted on our website.

Words: 0

Sample answer (Answer Key)

Turn To Page 30
Is this the way your lessons used to start at school? Did it spark your interest? I would say that the answer is probably no.

Not surprisingly, many teachers know their subjects well. The thing is this doesn’t seem to be enough. I have no doubt that you can remember a teacher who knew their stuff but couldn’t capture the attention of the class.

There has to be an additional skill. Getting students’ interest and participation is key. It’s good to do something practical: an experiment, a game, a role play. In fact, anything that makes the subject memorable means you’re halfway there.

Why is this important as long as you get the exams you need? Well, for one, it’s a more enjoyable experience for everyone. There is interaction and the belief that learning doesn’t have to be boring. What’s more, the student is more likely to want to study that subject to a higher level.

The evidence appears to support my opinion. You never forget a teacher who inspired you and that may be the reason why you’re doing what you do today.
