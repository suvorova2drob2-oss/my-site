The task

You have seen this announcement in an international magazine. Read it, then put the model paragraphs in order on the next step.

WHY ARE PEOPLE SO NOSTALGIC FOR THE PAST?

As we look to the future, so many people like to look back to where they’ve come from. What are the reasons for this love of our past lives? And why does it sometimes seem so much better than the present? The three best articles will be published in next month’s magazine.

Don’t forget!
  1. Begin with an interesting opening paragraph.
  2. Include direct questions and/or direct speech for a lively article.
  3. Use contractions and phrasal verbs for an informal style.
  4. Include a range of descriptive language and linking words.
  5. Leave the reader something to think about at the end.
  6. Give your article a catchy title.

5 Write your article

Write 140–190 words. Structure + useful language stay beside you.

UNHAPPY WITH YOUR LIFESTYLE? CHANGE IT!

What would make your life better? Think of two changes that would have a positive effect on your life. The three best articles will be published in next month’s magazine.

Useful language

Addressing the reader

  • You wouldn’t believe …
  • Have you ever …?
  • What would life be like if …
  • You absolutely must …
  • Just imagine …
  • I’m sure you’d agree …

Attitude adverbs

  • Importantly, …
  • Surprisingly, …
  • Essentially, …
  • Suddenly, …
  • Amazingly, …
  • Incredibly, …

Developing points

  • Let’s start with …
  • On top of all that, …
  • What’s more, …
  • Above all, …
  • Another advantage of …
  • Yet another reason is …
