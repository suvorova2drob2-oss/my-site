Correcting mistakes

In each short text 1–5, there are two words which should not be there. Click those words. The first one has been done for you.

1 At first we weren’t sure whether we could afford to go on holiday, but in the end we felt we ought to spend at least a week on the coast.

2 I was extremely impressed with the special effects and some of the action scenes. As for the acting, though, I felt many amateurs could have done better.

3 Sophie was so pleased after her last exam. ‘At last!’ she cried. ‘I’ve finished.’

4 When he came home from work he made himself a cup of tea and read the newspaper. It had been an exhausting day.

5 I’m so glad we took your advice and went to the new Indian restaurant that’s just opened. The service was marvellous and it was such good food.

Only the extra words are clickable. Example 1 is already crossed out: had, during.
