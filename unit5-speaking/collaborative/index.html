Useful language

Following rules

  • It is important/essential/necessary for them to follow/obey the rules, otherwise …
  • The rules are designed/intended to …
  • If they break the rules, they will/might (not) …

Asking questions

  • Can you think of any other reasons?
  • Do you agree with me on that?
  • What about drivers / children at home?

Agreeing and disagreeing

  • That’s right/true.
  • I think so, too.
  • I agree (up to a point).
  • I completely disagree.
