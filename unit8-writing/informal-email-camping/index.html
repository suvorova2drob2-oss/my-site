1 Ben’s email + Cecile’s reply (choose the informal option)

You have received an email from your English-speaking friend Ben.

Hi
How’s it going? I’m thinking about going away to travel around the south of France in the summer holidays. Anyway, I’m not sure whether to go camping or stay in a youth hostel. I’m planning to go for about a month. What do you think is the best idea?
Love
Ben

Dear Ben

(1) It was great to hear from you.

(2) I wish I could come with you!

I think you have to think about a few things. Camping is cheaper (3) and there will be loads of campsites you’ll be able to stay at. (4) you’d have to carry around your tent and everything. That’s pretty heavy, isn’t it?

Youth hostels are an easy option. They’re more expensive and some book up really early so (5) . But they’ll have everything you need and you can travel light. So, if I were you, (6) the youth hostel idea. (7) , I know there’s one near here so I could ask for some info if you want.

Anyway, I should go now. I have a lecture soon and I’m going to be late if I don’t hurry. (8)

Lots of love
Cecile

4 Write to Will (140–190 words)

Hey Pablo

Sorry I haven’t been in touch. I’d love to stay with you in June. Can you let me know if you want me to pick up anything for you from England? Also, can you tell me the best way to get from the airport to your place? Are you going to have any time off while I’m there? It would be great to hang out together and do something fun.

Thanks
Will

Words: 0

Sample answer (Answer Key — Pablo)

Hey Will
Brilliant news that you’re coming to Barcelona. I’ll get my spare room ready for you.
If you could get me some English chocolate, I’d love that, and maybe some Marmite. I know you think I’m crazy for liking it! And I’ll pay you back when you get here.
As for getting to my place from the airport, it’s pretty simple. You just take the airport underground to the city centre. It takes about half an hour. But anyway, don’t worry, as it’s the weekend, I can come and pick you up. Just tell me the time.
I can get a couple of days off, so how about we go to some touristic places like the Sagrada Familia and the Picasso Museum? Then we can head to the beach in the evening when it’s a bit cooler. What do you reckon?
Anyway, I’ve got to go or I’ll be late for this birthday party I’m going to.
Write to me asap.
Pablo
