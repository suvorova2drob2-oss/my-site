2 Dependent prepositions

In the following sentence from the article, there is an example of a dependent preposition. Choose the correct preposition to complete the sentences.

Stories like Mulan and Kung Fu Panda are based on Chinese culture and traditional stories.

  1. 1. Sarah congratulated me / about / on passing my driving test.
  2. 2. My sister has been jealous / by / for my long hair ever since we were children.

  5. 5. Simon won the competition and he wouldn’t stop boasting / of / with / about it.
  6. 6. I don’t blame you / in / at / for being so frightened / at / of / on spiders – I am too!
  7. 7. The criminal was arrested / in / by / on the police / for / about / with robbing the bank.
  8. 8. I couldn’t stop laughing / for / by / at my dog’s reaction / about / to / for their reflection.
  9. 9. James found it difficult to concentrate / on / for / at doing his homework because he was anxious / for / about / with finishing.
