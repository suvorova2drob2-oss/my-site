Phrase bank

Full idiom · meaning in everyday English

in the same ballpark

Approximately the same amount or range (origin: baseball).

on target

On course to achieve something; proceeding as planned.

no holds barred

With no limits or restrictions; completely frank.

bowl someone over

To surprise or impress someone greatly (origin: cricket / bowling).

take it on the chin

To accept criticism or bad news without complaining (origin: boxing).
