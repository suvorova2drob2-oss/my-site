The task (Ex. 1)

You see this notice in an English-language magazine.

ARTICLES WANTED!
MY FAVOURITE SPORT

Write us an article telling us about your favourite sport. Why do you like it and what advice would you give to someone who wants to take it up?

The best articles will be published in this magazine.

SPEAK Work in pairs. Talk about what ideas you would include in your answer.

Write your article in 140–190 words.

How to go about it
  1. Decide which sport you are going to write about, then plan your answer. You could use the same paragraph plan as the model answer in Exercise 2.
  2. Begin your article with an interesting opening paragraph. You could start with an interesting fact, a surprising statement or a direct question, as in the model answer.
  3. Aim to hold the target reader’s attention. Use a lively, engaging style throughout the article. You could involve the reader by talking to them directly, as in the model answer.
  4. End with a statement or question which summarises your thoughts and/or leaves the reader something to think about.
  5. Give your article a title to attract the reader’s attention. You could write this when you have finished your article.

Involving the reader

  • So what is the attraction of …?
  • And don’t be put off by …

Reasons & benefits

  • There is no better way of …
  • It makes me feel on top of the world.
  • It’s a powerful addiction.
  • For me, whether I’m … or …
