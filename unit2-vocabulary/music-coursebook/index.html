3 SPEAK

Work in pairs. Discuss the questions in Exercise 2, giving as much detail as possible.

Discussion prompts (Ex. 2)

  1. Have you been to see a singer or a band perform in a live music venue?
  2. Who would you most like to see perform live on stage?
  3. Have you ever performed or spoken on stage in front of an audience?
  4. Can you play a tune on any instrument?
  5. Can you sing in tune or do people cover their ears when they hear you?
  6. Do you listen to songs that are in the charts or do you prefer less commercial music?
  7. When you create a playlist, how do you decide which songs to include on it?
