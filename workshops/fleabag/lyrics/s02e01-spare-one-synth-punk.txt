SPARE ONE · S2E1 · Engagement dinner · synth-punk · 130 bpm

Style (Suno):
synth-punk, electro punk, female vocals, punchy aggressive synth, 130 bpm, rebellious shout-sung chorus, UK, staccato rap verses, hook loop, no ballad, no autotune

Exclude: slow, acoustic, electropop ballad, 88 bpm, soft

[Verse 1 — Rap, tight]

Six months and counting,
The tension is mounting.
Time to go off the sauce,
Whatever the cost.

Hidden in the underbelly,
Watching the news on the telly.

Hey, fellow smoker,
Life is a joker.

Fair enough —
The city is rough.

Ghastly without help, I imagine,
Like a beast in a cage — savage.

I'll just slug it over,
Far away from Dover.

The prudish people here
Live in constant fear.

[Verse 2 — Rap, faster]

The street is crawling with nuns,
Polishing their guns.

It wasn't too much of a leap,
To fall fast asleep.

A needy waitress in red,
Counting words that you said.

Jump on that request,
Put your soul to the test.

Got a chunk of change,
The feeling is strange.

"I'd kill for one of those,"

You can't pay your problems away,

Man, you've been ages,

On this crazy trip.
