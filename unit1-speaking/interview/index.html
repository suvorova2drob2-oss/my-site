Work with a partner. Discuss the questions — out loud, not in your head.

Question 1 / 5

1

Open your mouth and answer out loud. Don’t whisper to yourself — speak so your partner can hear you.

Say more than yes/no — add a reason or a short example.

How to go about it
  • Do not answer just ‘yes’ or ‘no’ to the examiner’s questions. Develop your answers by giving reasons or examples.
  • Do not learn long pre-prepared answers. You are likely to sound unnatural and you may not answer the questions correctly.
