1 Essay question (main)

In your English class you have been talking about the world of work. Your English teacher has asked you to write an essay.

Write an essay using all the notes and giving reasons for your point of view.

It is better to work at home than in an office. Do you agree?

Notes — Things to write about:

  1. contact with people
  2. working hours
  3. … (your own idea)
